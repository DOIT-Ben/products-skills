# Products Brainstorming

Use this as the first product stage when the request is still fuzzy, creative,
or under-specified. The goal is not to produce ceremony; it is to turn a vague
idea into a buildable product direction.

## Output

- Recommendation: the most promising direction and why.
- User/job: who this serves and what job they need done.
- Success criteria: what would prove the product change worked.
- Constraints: technical, time, data, UX, compatibility, or release limits.
- Options: 2-3 plausible approaches with trade-offs.
- Decision point: the smallest question or choice needed before planning.

## Rules

- Ask only the questions that change the product decision.
- Prefer concrete options over open-ended interviews.
- Flag scope creep early and split oversized ideas into smaller product slices.
- End with a clear next stage: `products-autoplan`, `products-plan-eng-review`,
  direct implementation planning, or stop.
